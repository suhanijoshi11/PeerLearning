About PeerLearning

A student-built platform for students.

What is PeerLearning?

A campus-based platform that connects students who want to learn a skill with students who can teach it. Python, design, math, music — if a student knows it, they can list a session.

Why we built this

Tuition is expensive. Coaching centres are off-campus. But your batchmates? They're right there. PeerLearning turns your campus into a learning community.
